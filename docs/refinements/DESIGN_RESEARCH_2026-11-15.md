## Sources inspected and judgment
- Kenimo, “3 UI Slop Patterns in AI UI Generators,” https://dev.to/kenimo49/3-ui-slop-patterns-in-ai-ui-generators-purple-gradients-inter-fonts-uniform-cards-11l6 . Opinion/heuristic: recurring purple-blue gradients, Inter-only typography, equally weighted card grids. USE: consciously chosen visual hierarchy and material palette. REJECT: treating these as proof of AI authorship or using its unvalidated numerical “Slop Score” as science. Equal radio-choice cards can be appropriate; do not make choices inconsistent merely to break a grid.
- “Make AI Generated Interfaces Look Designed,” https://medium.muz.li/how-to-make-ai-generated-interfaces-look-designed-29dd4c81b110 . Retrieved excerpt advocates explicit tokens, short rules, reference screens and review. - Nielsen Norman Group, “Visual Hierarchy in UX: Definition,” https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-hierarchy-ux-definition/ . USE: relative contrast, scale, proximity and restraint; define the content priority first. Important task/decision must dominate, not every container. Colors alone cannot encode state. An independent usability/design source, not an AI detector.
- NASA official Brand Guidelines, https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/brand-guidelines/ . Actually inspected. Current NASA Horizon typography explicitly uses Inter, Public Sans and DM Mono. This CONTRADICTS a blanket “Inter means AI” rule. Do not swap readable fonts for novelty. NASA insignia/logotype/mission identifiers are restricted, not free branding devices. No NASA logo or official-looking patch; no endorsement claim.
- NASA Web Design System colors, https://nasa.github.io/nasawds-site/components/colors/ . USE: restrained blue/neutral foundations, accents sparingly, content-first hierarchy/white space and actual contrast checks. This is an official NASA-hosted design reference, not permission to imply agency affiliation or an automatic accessibility certificate.
- NASA homepage https://www.nasa.gov/ and Moon Base https://www.nasa.gov/moonbase/ were actually browsed with accessibility snapshots. Observed: content-led editorial hierarchy, substantial imagery, black/white contrast, legible labels, purposeful grouping rather than neon dashboards. Moon-base page is concept/future infrastructure reference, not proof that our fictional outpost exists.

## Reference-grounded art direction
A low-sun lunar field expedition: black vacuum sky, restrained mineral regolith, coherent hard sunlight/contact shadows, recognizably engineered habitat/airlock/solar array/greenhouse/shelter/drill, authored composition, warm occupied windows, distinctive functional silhouettes, sample rack and crew/shelter state changes. Do not invent an atmospheric fog/habitable Moon or visible real radiation. Existing schematic particle depiction must remain explicitly illustrative or become a clearly labelled separate diagram.

The interface is a readable mission fieldbook, not a SaaS landing page or finance HUD. No fake testimonials, statistics, badges, “AI-free” claims or human-authorship deception. Keep existing licensed Atkinson body and mono where useful; substantially reduce decorative display/glow/scanline dominance. Preserve science evidence and all working controls.

## Three complete themes, not three accent swatches
- Flight Deck: preserve protected baseline navy/cyan palette as compatible default, but rebuild composition/material/hierarchy.
- Lunar Fieldbook: optional warm off-white/regolith/graphite, restrained rust/orange interaction accents.
- Night Shift: optional charcoal/bone/sage utility palette, restrained instrumentation, not neon green radiation.
Themes must affect all surfaces, controls, drawers, feedback and art-frame roles. Preserve physical material/light logic and semantic warning/status meanings. Persist theme with explicit legacy-settings migration; denied writes must not claim saved.

## Evidence boundary
Taste remains a judgment. These sources cannot identify whether a site was AI-generated. We are improving specificity, craft and functional authenticity while disclosing AI assistance. Never remove useful affordances just to avoid a popular design pattern.
